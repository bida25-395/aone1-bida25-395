Rooted in Tradition

Grown with Botho, Harvested for Kagiso.

At Mercy Door Farms, our philosophy is simple: we treat our soil with the same respect we show our neighbors. Botho — the essence of being human — guide every seed we plant and every hand that harvests.

Founded in the heart of Botswana, we've transformed ancestral wisdom into modern sustainable practices. Our mission is to provide peace (Kagiso) to your home through food that is safe, pure, and honestly grown.
